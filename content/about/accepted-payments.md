At The Firelight Studio, payment is due at the time services are rendered. We offer a wide variety of convenient payment options to make your checkout seamless, transparent, and flexible.

### Insurance & HSA / FSA Accounts
While we do not bill insurance directly, medical massage therapy is often reimbursable through Health Savings Accounts (HSA) and Flexible Spending Accounts (FSA). Upon request, we can provide detailed itemized Superbill receipts containing all requisite taxonomy codes, diagnosis codes, and provider NPI numbers for you to submit to your insurer or plan administrator for direct reimbursement.
