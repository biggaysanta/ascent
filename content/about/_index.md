Welcome, and thank you for your interest - I hope you'll decide to work with me.

I've been practicing therapeutic massage and since 2003, after a 20 computer programming career.  I wanted to do something physical, but still be able to put my analytic, problem-solving skills to continued use.  Friends always said I had good hands, so it seems like a perfect fit.  And well, all these years later, it still is.

I invite you to come and experience the difference between my work and other massage therapists.  You'll know it by the results.

I love you all, and I bid you peace.
