I value **communication over non-communication**, and am willing to be somewhat flexible with clients once they have an established relationship with me. But in general, these are my appointment policies:

#### Cancellation Policy

If you must cancel an appointment, you have 24 hours before the start time of the appointment to cancel without paying a cancellation fee. If you cancel the appointment less than 24 hours, you will pay the entire session fee from the card on file.

#### No Show Policy

It's the same for being a no-show:
If you do not show up for your appointment by the 15 minute late mark, I will consider you a no-show, and you will pay the entire session fee from the card I have on file for you.

#### Rescheduling Policy

If you need to reschedule, you have until 4 hours before the start time of the appointment.  After that, it'll be a late reschedule fee of 50% of session fee.

#### Late Policy

Sessions start on time, and end on time. If I am late, the session will be extended by the amount of my lateness, or if not possible to extend, the late time will be added on to the next session.

#### Secret Handshake Club Members

If you are a member of the Secret Handshake Club, these policies also apply to you. I will bill you as above, but at the discounted rate.  You will not lose your member benefit massage, and may still redeem it as long as the billing period has not closed.
