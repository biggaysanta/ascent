Tidal Wave Relaxation Massage

Just like a tidal wave, which wipes out everything in its path, my unique Tidal Wave Relaxation Massage, so let’s talk about it!

How is it unique?

Well, the session is all performed with the handshapes of Swedish Massage, open hands, and a soft fist. There are no elbows or “deep” tissue work. But don’t think that this is namby-pamby massage. You’ll be so relaxed, I’ll likely pour you out of my office.

Special Draping Technique

The session is performed on left and right sides individually. Because of this, I had to develop a special draping technique - one that maintains your modesty yet allows me access to all of the normal areas a massage therapist would access: feet, legs, glutes, back, shoulders, neck, arms and hands. And when face up, abdominal muscles and (limited) pectoral muscles.

Technique: Top Secret!

No, really, it’s very rhythmic and global, punctuated with regional work that is integrated with the global work.

The result? Stress wiped away!

Except instead of devastation in its wake, this Tidal Wave will leave you both relaxed and energized, awake and ready to sleep deeply that night.

Session Info

I offer the Tidal Wave Relaxation Massage in three lengths: 60, 90, and 120 minute lengths, at a flat rate of $135 per hour.
